Distribution used to mean getting in front of a person: a search result, a conference talk, a well-timed tweet. Now a growing share of your distribution runs through models that suggest, scaffold, and import on the developer's behalf. If the model defaults to a competitor, you are invisible at the exact moment the choice is made.

Getting to be that default is a discipline, and it is not the same as SEO.

## Three surfaces that decide the default

**Retrieval.** When an agent needs a tool for your category, what does it pull? Models lean on what is well-represented and unambiguous in their training and retrieval. Owning a clear category noun ("the Postgres backend," "the cross-platform graphics engine") gives the model a clean handle to reach for. Fuzzy positioning makes you unretrievable.

**Citation.** When a model explains how to do the thing, does it name you? This is downstream of how consistently and clearly your approach is described across the open web: your docs, yes, but also the answers, tutorials, and posts that reference them. Being the canonical explanation of a problem is how you become the canonical tool for it.

**Import.** The final mile. When code actually gets written, is it `import you` or `import them`? This is won by everything in your quickstart being correct, current, and the path of least resistance. The default import is the one that just works when a model tries it.

## Make yourself the easy answer

A few concrete moves:

- **Ship an `llms.txt` and treat your docs as a machine-read surface,** not just a human one. Structure, stable URLs, and unambiguous naming beat clever copy.
- **Publish the canonical example for your category** and make it so clean that other people's tutorials copy it. Their content becomes your distribution.
- **Reduce the number of correct ways to use your tool to one.** Optionality confuses models. A single blessed path gets reproduced faithfully.
- **Keep your public surface consistent over time.** Every rename, every breaking change, resets the model's confidence and hands the default back to whoever held still.

## The mindset shift

For years we optimized to be *discovered* by people who were looking. The new job is to be *assumed* by models that are building. Discovery is a moment of attention you have to win again and again. Being the default is a position you hold until someone actively unseats you. Get there first, then be boring enough to stay.
