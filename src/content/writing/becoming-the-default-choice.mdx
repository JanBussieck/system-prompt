Distribution used to mean getting in front of a person. A search result, a conference talk, a well-timed tweet. A growing share of it now runs through models that suggest, scaffold, and import on the developer's behalf, and if the model defaults to a competitor you're invisible at the exact moment the choice gets made.

Becoming that default is its own discipline, and it isn't SEO.

## Three surfaces that decide the default

### Retrieval

When an agent needs a tool for your category, what does it pull? Models lean on whatever is well represented and unambiguous in their training and retrieval. A clear category noun, "the Postgres backend" or "the cross-platform graphics engine," gives the model a clean handle to reach for. Fuzzy positioning makes you unretrievable.

### Citation

When a model explains how to do the thing, does it name you? That depends on how consistently your approach is described across the open web: your docs, but also the answers, tutorials, and posts that reference them. Become the canonical explanation of a problem and you become the canonical tool for it.

### Import

When code actually gets written, is it `import you` or `import them`? You win this by making everything in your quickstart correct, current, and easier than the alternatives. The default import is the one that works when a model tries it.

## Make yourself the easy answer

A few concrete moves:

- Ship an `llms.txt` and treat your docs as a machine-read surface, not only a human one. Structure, stable URLs, and unambiguous naming beat clever copy.
- Publish the canonical example for your category, clean enough that other people's tutorials copy it. Their content becomes your distribution.
- Reduce the number of correct ways to use your tool to one. Optionality confuses models, and a single blessed path gets reproduced faithfully.
- Hold your public surface still. Every rename and every breaking change resets the model's confidence and hands the default to whoever didn't move.

## The mindset shift

For years we optimized to be *discovered* by people who were looking. The job now is to be *assumed* by models that are building. Discovery is attention you win again and again. A default is a position you keep until someone unseats you, which argues for getting there early and then being boring about it.
